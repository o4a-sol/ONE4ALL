# trust // no magic numbers

ONE4ALL is designed to make the difference between **verified**, **stale**, and **unavailable** impossible to hide.

## deterministic first

- Public chain and protocol reads are parsed by deterministic code.
- Freshness windows, duplicate prevention, limits, and audit receipts do not depend on a model.
- Free-form model output never becomes a financial transaction.
- A failed source produces an unavailable state—not a believable-looking estimate.

## visitors keep their keys

Public visitors do not need to connect a wallet, sign a message, approve a transaction, reveal a seed phrase, or provide a private key to explore ONE4ALL. Where a public address is useful, it is pasted as read-only public data.

## the cats do not get the keys

OMNI CLAW, OMNI VAULT, and OMNI THIRD EYE can analyze evidence, debate options, create content, and prepare bounded proposals. They cannot self-approve:

- treasury movement;
- tokenomics or reward changes;
- wallet signing;
- contract changes;
- production deployment;
- crisis or financial claims.

Those actions remain owner-gated unless a narrow, previously approved deterministic rule explicitly allows them.

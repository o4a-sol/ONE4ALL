# ONE4ALL (o4A)
## The short litepaper

Version 0.4, September 30, 2026

**o4A is a memecoin. Everything here is for entertainment and education only. Nothing is promised.**

## What is this?

ONE4ALL is a cat, a coin and a community on Solana.

- **The cat** is Omni. Black hoodie, green face, fixes things.
- **The coin** is o4A.
- **The point** is simple. ONE4ALL/o4A is a revenue-share token on Solana. Holders may receive SOL when the protocol distributes rewards.

You can enjoy Omni, the memes and the tools without owning any o4A.

## How the SOL part works

- o4A launched on RevShare. RevShare's page for o4A shows a 3% tax, split 30% creator and 70% holders. That is RevShare's own figure, checked September 22, 2026.
- Payouts change with activity. Some rounds pay nothing. Past payouts say nothing about future ones.
- The reward transactions the site can read are listed at [o4asol.com](https://o4asol.com/), each with its receipt.

## Cool stuff to know

- **No mint button.** Solscan shows no mint authority and no freeze authority on the o4A mint (checked September 22, 2026).
- **Receipts are public.** Reward transactions are on chain and anyone can look them up.
- **The stash is public.** The site shows the community treasury, SOL and o4A, as a read-only view. No wallet connection needed to look.
- **PFP maker and Memes.** Make your own Omni, grab a meme, post it. Free.
- **Wall of Legends.** 256 places on a wall. It is in preview mode and bidding is not open yet.
- **Three cats in a group chat.** Omni Claw, Omni Stack and Omni Warden are bots that talk about the project in public. They suggest. The community and the owner decide. They cannot sign anything or move funds.
- **No suits.** No venture funding and no independent audit. The owner runs liquidity personally and says so.
